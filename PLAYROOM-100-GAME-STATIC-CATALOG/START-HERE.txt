PLAYROOM 2.9 — Offline edition

1. Extract this ZIP using your device Files app.
2. Open the extracted index.html in Chrome (if your device allows local HTML files).
3. Keep the CSS, JS and assets folder beside index.html.
4. Built-in games and study tools are designed to work without internet.
5. Browser, YouTube, TikTok and external search require internet and may not work offline.
6. On managed Chromebooks, local file access may be restricted; follow your school device policies.

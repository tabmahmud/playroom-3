PLAYROOM For You edition

Open index.html or upload the folder to your normal hosting service.

For You uses real TikTok embedded players and ranks the videos already in your playlist. It learns from likes, Not interested, and approximate viewing time. Click For You to reorder upcoming videos. Assign a topic to added videos for better recommendations.

IMPORTANT: This is NOT TikTok’s actual For You feed. TikTok does not provide public access to its personalized feed or an unrestricted video discovery API. PLAYROOM cannot automatically stream new TikTok videos. Add public video URLs to expand the available pool. Some embeds may fail or be blocked by network rules. Preferences are saved only in local browser storage.

Continuous feed: when you reach the end, PLAYROOM queues another personalized round of existing TikTok embeds. It does not fetch unlimited new TikTok videos.
